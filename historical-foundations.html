Project 0 Documentation: Before launching our scaled audio-visual advocacy and clinical synchronization models, the Local Urban Forum Network conducted foundational grassroots mapping across Nairobi's informal settlements. This diagnostic phase established the empirical baseline for our 9-Pillar framework.

Key Baseline Findings & Diagnostic Tracks

1. Unmasked Neuropsychiatric & Primary Health Gaps

Early field observations revealed severe barriers to psychiatric triage, epilepsy medication continuity, and stigma-free counseling within high-density urban sectors. These diagnostic findings directly shaped our clinical referral architecture and institutional alignment with Mathari National Teaching & Referral Hospital.

2. Environmental & Urban Drainage Vulnerability

Grassroots transect walks and community listening sessions documented how solid polymer waste accumulation in neighborhood drainage channels directly triggers localized flash flooding. This baseline data informed our Circular Plastics Initiative and our 3-person responder vulnerability mapping protocols.

3. Household Economic Strain & Civic Exclusion

Initial household surveys highlighted how acute financial shocks disproportionately impact women- and youth-led households, compounding mental health stressors and limiting civic participation. These insights anchored our Maternal Micro-Grant tracks and digital literacy bootcamps.

Transition to Active Practice & 2027 Rollout

The raw qualitative stories and sector mapping logs collected during this foundational phase were systematically digitized to create the Mtaa Digital Ledger. Today, this historical baseline serves as the control benchmark against which all active field interventions and our upcoming 2027–2030 multi-pillar programs are evaluated.
