Project 0 Institutional Archive (2010–2025): Prior to structuring our scaled audio-visual advocacy and clinical synchronization models, the Local Urban Forum Network (LUF Network) and our sister initiative, Solidarity Kenya, conducted over a decade of grassroots field diagnostics across Nairobi, Kisumu, Mombasa, and central urban corridors. This empirical groundwork established the baseline for our 9-Pillar framework.

Key Baseline Findings & Diagnostic Tracks

2010 – 2018 | Nairobi, Kisumu & Mombasa

1. Community Health Interventions & Neuropsychiatric Care Gaps

Early grassroots health reporting—spanning community HIV/AIDS interventions and primary care monitoring across Nairobi (including Kibra and Shaurimoyo), Kisumu, and Mombasa—revealed severe diagnostic gaps in chronic neurological and psychiatric care. These historical field observations directly shaped our frontline psychiatric safety protocols and institutional referral alignment with Mathari National Teaching & Referral Hospital.

2013 – 2023 | Nyeri, Ruiru, Nakuru & Nairobi

2. Citizens' Forums, Civic Participation & Urban Resilience

Beginning in 2013 with the establishment of structured citizens' forums in Nyeri, Ruiru, and Nakuru, our teams mapped how inadequate urban governance and uncollected solid polymer waste compound flash-flood risks in dense settlements. By 2023, cross-border field diagnostics on migration governance and urban vulnerability solidified the blueprint for our Circular Plastics Initiative and 3-person responder tracking teams.

2024 – 2026 | Kibra & Shaurimoyo Sectors

3. Household Economic Strain & Structural Equity Diagnostics

Targeted household surveys and listening sessions across Nairobi's informal settlements highlighted how acute financial shocks disproportionately affect women- and youth-led households, compounding mental health stressors and limiting civic agency. These localized baselines directly anchored our Maternal Micro-Grant tracks and youth digital literacy bootcamps.

Transition to Active Practice & February 2027 Rollout

The raw qualitative stories, citizens' forum resolutions, and sector mapping logs collected between 2010 and 2026 were systematically digitized to create the Mtaa Digital Ledger. Today, this historical archive serves as the verified baseline benchmark against which all active field interventions and our upcoming February 2027 – 2030 multi-pillar programs are evaluated.
