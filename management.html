🔄 Closed-Loop Institutional Synergy (Why This Structure Delivers)

This 4-tier architecture eliminates the gap between fiduciary governance and frontline impact: Tom Onzere, Goretty Yogo, Youth Chapter Leads (Risper Akinyi & Brian Odongo), and 3-Person Responder Teams capture verified household needs and execute ward-level interventions; Technical & Clinical Leads (Dr. Olivia Onditi, Dr. Joseph Olewe, and Stephen Ongondo) audit outcomes via the Mtaa Digital Ledger and synthesize scalable evidence; and our Executive Secretariat & Board convert verified metrics into high-compliance international and commercial consortium partnerships.
