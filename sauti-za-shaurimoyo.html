Project 1: Sauti za Shaurimoyo (Evidence Ledger)

Focus Matrix: Community-Driven Audio-Visual Storytelling & Climate Resilience.
Operational Space: Shaurimoyo and surrounding high-density informal settlements, Nairobi.
Core Objective: Utilizing community media and oral histories to influence local adaptation frameworks.
📖 The Narrative Horizon: From Oral History to Structural Change

"Behind every data cluster listed on this ledger sits a lived reality. During the extreme weather shocks hitting Nairobi’s high-density areas, community voice logs captured a striking pattern: while standard environmental indicators logged flood damage to physical property, our audio recorders captured a deeper crisis of livelihoods. Local women-led micro-enterprises and small-scale urban agriculture networks were systematically wiped out overnight. By capturing these spoken archives, we didn't just log data—we weaponized memories to design local advocacy strategies. This narrative tracking led directly to the creation of communal pacts with local Chiefs and Nyumba Kumi elders, establishing local adaptation networks that bridge the gap between county climate policies and real everyday lives."
