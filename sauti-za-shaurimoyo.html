Project 1: Sauti za Shaurimoyo (Evidence Ledger)

Focus Matrix: Community-Driven Audio-Visual Storytelling & Climate Resilience.
Operational Space: Shaurimoyo and surrounding high-density informal settlements, Nairobi.
Core Objective: Utilizing community media and oral histories to influence local adaptation frameworks.

📂 Campaign Artifacts & Public Transcripts

Audio Archive

Community Oral History Repositories (Tape 01-08)

Verbatim digital recordings documenting multi-generational displacement timelines, drainage failures during extreme weather shocks, and grassroots response mechanisms inside informal sectors.

Media Campaign

Grassroots Policy Briefs & Audio-Visual Screenings

Documentation of localized town hall feedback frames utilizing multimedia projections to bridge communications between sub-county environmental desk heads and community clusters.

Data Model

Open-Source Community Adaptation Matrix

Publicly verifiable spreadsheet cataloging environmental risk layers, emergency structural shelter spaces, and structural vector pathways managed directly by our grassroots responder blocks.
