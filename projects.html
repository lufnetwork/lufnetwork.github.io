Pillar 9: Research Log

Baseline Health Tracking & Community Insights

Active Field Evaluation Hub

Conducting localized baseline data compilation, health stigma surveys, and resource accessibility mapping across congested informal hubs. This tracking enables us to output transparent data streams that fuel national agency compliance and clinic coordination.

📋 Active Field Survey Trackers

Our field mapping teams collect standardized data across urban sectors to evaluate hidden health stress metrics using these four primary research anchors:

  • M1: Resource Proximity Index — "What is the physical travel time and exact financial cost required for an individual in this household to reach the nearest primary healthcare clinic?"
  • M2: Healthcare Stigma Barrier — "Has fear of community isolation or institutional discrimination prevented anyone in this household from seeking counseling or treatment in the last 6 months?"
  • M3: Behavioral Stress Curvature — "Are household members noticing an escalation of substance dependency or acute economic distress symptoms among young adults in their immediate circle?"
  • M4: Compliance & Follow-up Path — "For previously referred individuals, what are the primary structural barriers preventing strict compliance with clinic appointment tracks?"
