Grassroots Activism

Grassroots Mental Health Frontlines

Specialized Community-Led Coalitions & Burden Framework

The Burden of Illness: Mental health, behavioral, and social problems are an increasing part of our public health burden. Hundreds of thousands of women, men, and children suffer silently from mental illness, while countless others experience deep distress from the consequences of violence, dislocation, poverty, and exploitation.

Demographic Vulnerability: Substance abuse and deep-rooted behavioral problems aggressively affect the lives of countless men, adolescents, young adults, middle-aged women, and the elderly across urban hubs.

The Hidden Statistic: Though the systemic burden resulting from psychiatric and behavioral disorders is enormous, it is grossly under-represented by conventional public health statistics. Furthermore, individuals who suffer from mental illnesses are severely stigmatized, which deeply extends to their families.

Mental health is a vital, non-negotiable component of the entire health status of an individual.
