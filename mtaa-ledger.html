Project 3: Mtaa Digital Ledger (Cross-Cutting Civic Infrastructure)

Focus Matrix: Open Governance, Digital Archiving & Institutional Compliance.
Operational Space: Sub-county coordination hubs (Mathare North, Huruma, Baba Dogo), Nairobi.
Core Objective: Restructuring field updates into structured, Excel-compatible databases for progress reporting.
📖 The Narrative Horizon: Systematizing Grassroots Accountability

"A creative practice only achieves long-term structural impact when its operational backbone is transparent and standardized. The Mtaa Digital Ledger serves as the centralized clearinghouse where human narrative maps meet quantitative tracking. Every single action taken by our decentralized hubs is run through this tracking pipeline. By formatting raw, field-level interactions into structured CSV frameworks, we eliminate administrative lag and ensure our project reporting tracks are completely audit-ready. This everyday operational discipline provides international evaluation boards with an open verification channel, bridging real field execution directly with donor compliance baselines."
